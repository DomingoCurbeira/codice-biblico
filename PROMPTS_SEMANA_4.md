# 🎬 Prompts Cinematográficos - Semana 4 (El Despertar de la Voz)
**Dirección de Arte (Core):** Ultra-realismo oscuro (gritty), iluminación dramática de claroscuro (chiaroscuro), paleta de colores desaturada (bronce, ámbar, carbón, sombras profundas), textura de película 35mm, enfoque en la intensidad psicológica y la textura de los materiales (polvo, lino antiguo, piedra).

---

### 1. Ana: El Grito de la Esterilidad (Lunes)
**Archivo:** `ana-altar.webp\'
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic wide shot, extreme realism. A broken, weeping ancient Hebrew woman (Hannah) kneeling on the dusty stone floor of a dimly lit, rustic ancient sanctuary (Shiloh). She is wearing simple, rough linen garments. Her face is buried in her hands, shoulders shaking in silent agony. In the background, out of focus, an overweight high priest (Eli) sitting on a wooden chair, watching her suspiciously. Atmospheric lighting, shafts of sunlight piercing through wooden slats, dust particles in the air. Deep shadows, emotional, gritty texture, chiaroscuro, 8k resolution, historical accuracy, masterpiece.

---

### 2. El Mito de la Piedad por Herencia (Martes)
**Archivo:** `eli-silo.webp\'
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic medium shot, dark fantasy realism. Two arrogant, corrupt ancient Hebrew priests (Hophni and Phinehas) standing in front of a smoking bronze altar. They are wearing ornate but slightly dirty priestly garments. One is holding a large, wicked-looking iron meat hook (trident). Their expressions are greedy and irreverent. In the dark background, the faint golden glow of the Ark of the Covenant behind a veil. Gritty atmosphere, smoke, flying embers, cinematic lighting, shadow-heavy, bronze and dark amber color palette, highly detailed textures, 35mm film aesthetic.

---

### 3. Shamá': El Arte de Escuchar (Miércoles)
**Archivo:** `shama-oir.webp\'
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic close-up, extreme realism. A young ancient Hebrew boy (Samuel, around 10 years old) waking up in the middle of the night. He is sitting up on a simple woven mat on a stone floor. He is wearing a plain white linen tunic. His eyes are wide open, staring into the darkness with a mix of awe and slight fear. The environment is pitch black, illuminated only by a single, soft, warm, supernatural golden light source hitting his face from off-camera. Emotional intensity, deep shadows, gritty, chiaroscuro lighting, highly detailed eyes reflecting the light, masterpiece, 8k.

---

### 4. Silo: El Santuario Olvidado (Jueves)
**Archivo:** `silo-ruinas.webp\'
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic landscape, dark realism. The smoldering ruins of an ancient Hebrew sanctuary (Shiloh) immediately after a devastating battle. Destroyed stone walls, collapsed wooden pillars, and torn, burnt linen curtains blowing in the wind. The ground is covered in ash and debris. Plumes of thick, dark smoke rising into a twilight sky. A sense of tragic emptiness and divine abandonment (Ichabod). Gritty texture, desolate atmosphere, desaturated colors, ash-gray and burnt orange tones, highly detailed, photorealistic, epic composition.

### 5. Samuel: El Oído que Restauró la Palabra (Viernes)
**Archivo:** `samuel-noche.webp\'
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic portrait, gritty realism. An older, battle-hardened ancient Hebrew prophet (adult Samuel) standing on a rocky hill at dusk. He has a long, unkempt gray beard, weathered skin, and piercing, authoritative eyes. He is wearing a heavy, rough wool cloak. He is holding a shofar (ram's horn) in one hand. The wind is blowing his hair. The sky behind him is dramatic, filled with heavy, dark storm clouds, with a single beam of light breaking through. Epic, authoritative, chiaroscuro lighting, highly textured skin, cinematic color grading, 8k.
