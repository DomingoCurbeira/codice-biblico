# 🎬 Prompts Cinematográficos - Semana 6 (El Reino de la Apariencia)
**Dirección de Arte (Core):** Ultra-realismo oscuro (gritty), iluminación dramática de claroscuro (chiaroscuro), paleta de colores desaturada (bronce, ámbar, carbón, sombras profundas), textura de película 35mm, enfoque en la intensidad psicológica y la textura de los materiales (polvo, lino antiguo, piedra).

---

### 1. Saúl: El Rey que el Pueblo eligió (Lunes)
**Archivo:** `saul-ungimiento.webp`
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic medium shot, gritty realism. An ancient Hebrew prophet (Samuel), with a long grey beard and worn linen robe, pouring oil from a ram's horn onto the head of a tall, imposing young man (Saul). Saul has a strong, muscular build and handsome features, but his eyes show deep hidden insecurity and hesitation. The scene is set in an old stone room with dramatic chiaroscuro lighting. Oil glimmers as it runs down Saul's dark hair and face. Muted tones, warm bronze and charcoal colors, highly detailed skin textures, emotional intensity, 35mm film aesthetic, masterpiece.

---

### 2. El Mito de la Impaciencia Justificada (Martes)
**Archivo:** `saul-sacrificio.webp`
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic wide shot, dark epic realism. At the camp of Gilgal, a crude stone altar is engulfed in heavy smoke from a burning sacrifice. The tall King Saul stands beside it, wearing a simple bronze crown and leather armor, his hands covered in soot. His face is filled with anxiety, panic, and impatience, looking over his shoulder as if waiting for someone. In the background, Hebrew soldiers look fearful and discouraged, preparing for a looming battle. Overcast sky, dusty atmosphere, dramatic shadows, muted earth tones, gritty textures of stone and leather, chiaroscuro, 8k resolution.

---

### 3. Qésem: Adivinación y Rebelión (Miércoles)
**Archivo:** `endor-nigromante.webp`
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic medium shot, dark occult realism. Inside a dark, smoky cavern lit only by a small, flickering fire. A desperate King Saul, disguised in a simple, dark hooded cloak, looks on with absolute terror and dread. Across the fire, an old, mysterious medium (witch of Endor) with intense eyes performs a forbidden ritual, her hands raised over rising smoke. Faintly emerging from the thick smoke is a spectral, ghostly, and solemn figure of the old prophet Samuel. High psychological tension, heavy shadows, chiaroscuro lighting, deep blacks, gritty and ancient textures, 35mm film grain, masterpiece.

---

### 4. Gabaa de Saúl: El Palacio Rústico (Jueves)
**Archivo:** `gabaa-saul.webp`
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic wide shot, gritty historical realism. A modest, rustic stone fortress (Gibeah) standing on a windy, barren hill under a stormy, dark sky. The architecture is simple and rough, made of unpolished stones and heavy wooden beams, resembling a military outpost rather than a grand palace. A few sentries stand on the low walls. Through a stone archway, King Saul can be seen sitting alone on a simple wooden seat, looking paranoid and isolated. Cold, moody lighting, desaturated colors, highly detailed textures of rough stone and dry grass, 8k resolution.

---

### 5. El Espíritu que se retira (Viernes)
**Archivo:** `saul-lanza.webp`
**Prompt:**
> Cinematic close-up, intense psychological horror realism. Inside a dark, stone royal chamber. King Saul is consumed by madness and jealousy, his eyes wide and bloodshot, filled with paranoia. He is clutching a heavy bronze spear (javelin) tightly, ready to throw it. The lighting is extremely dramatic chiaroscuro, casting long, menacing shadows on his face, highlighting his sweat-sheened skin and disheveled hair. The atmosphere feels heavy and oppressive, reflecting his inner torment. Dark, desaturated colors, gritty texture, masterpiece, 8k resolution.
